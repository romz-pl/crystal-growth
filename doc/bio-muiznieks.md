# Andris Muižnieks (Andris Muiznieks): An Exhaustive Scientific Biography

**University of Latvia — Faculty of Physics and Mathematics / Institute of Solid State Physics**
**Deceased 5 April 2013**

---

## 1. Overview

Andris Muižnieks (also transliterated **Andris Muiznieks** in the international literature) was a Latvian theoretical/computational physicist at the University of Latvia who became one of the world's leading figures in the mathematical modeling of industrial silicon single-crystal growth. Working at the interface of electrodynamics, magnetohydrodynamics (MHD), heat transfer, and continuum mechanics, he built — largely in a decades-long partnership with Alfred Mühlbauer and later Bernard Nacke of the Institute of Electrothermal Process Engineering at Leibniz Universität Hannover, and with industrial partners Wacker Siltronic AG (later Siltronic AG) and Wacker Chemie AG — the numerical and physical modeling foundations that underpin present-day simulation of the **Czochralski (CZ)** and **Floating Zone (FZ)** processes for growing large-diameter silicon crystals.

He held his academic base at the **University of Latvia**, principally in the **Faculty of Physics and Mathematics** (Chair/Department of Electrodynamics and Continuum Mechanics, and later the Department of Physics) and in the **Institute of Solid State Physics, University of Latvia (ISSP UL)**, while conducting extended collaborative research at the **Leibniz Universität Hannover** (Institute of Electrothermal Process Engineering) in Germany. He died on 5 April 2013, in the midst of active research; several of his papers and one major handbook chapter were completed or published posthumously by his students and collaborators.

## 3. Scientific Career and Research Program

### 3.1 Foundational period: electromagnetic field modeling for induction heating (early–mid 1990s)

Muižnieks's earliest documented publications, from the early 1990s, were produced jointly with Alfred Mühlbauer of the Universität Hannover and dealt with the **numerical calculation of electromagnetic fields in induction furnaces**, including cold-crucible induction melting systems — foundational work for what would become his crystal-growth specialty:

- Mühlbauer, A.; Muiznieks, A.; Jakowitsch, A., "Modellierung des elektromagnetischen Feldes in Induktionsöfen mit kaltem Tiegel," *Elektrowärme International B*, 49 (1991), 130–141.
- Muiznieks, A.; Westphal, E.; Mühlbauer, A., "Modellierung des elektromagnetischen Feldes in Induktionsöfen mit einem dickwandigen metallischen Tiegel," *elektrowärme international*, 50 (1992), B3, B286–B294.
- Westphal, E.; Mühlbauer, A.; Muiznieks, A., "Berechnung von elektromagnetischen Feldern in zylindrischen Induktionssystemen mit periodisch geschlitzten Wänden," *Electrical Engineering (Archiv für Elektrotechnik)*, 77 (1994), 157–168.
- Mühlbauer, A.; Muiznieks, A.; Jakowitsch, A.; Virbulis, J., "Berechnung des dreidimensionalen Hochfrequenzfeldes beim Zonenschmelzen von Silizium," *Archiv für Elektrotechnik*, 76 (1993), 161–168.

This period established his signature methodology: coupling boundary-element and edge-finite-element techniques for high-frequency electromagnetic (EM) field calculation with thermal and hydrodynamic models of the melt — a combination he would apply for the rest of his career to industrial-scale silicon crystal growth.

### 3.2 Czochralski silicon growth: melt convection under magnetic fields (mid-1990s–2000s)

From the mid-1990s, Muižnieks turned this EM-field expertise toward the **Czochralski process for large-diameter silicon crystal growth**, in direct partnership with Wacker Siltronic AG. His central contribution was the coupled modeling of **turbulent melt convection under the influence of externally applied magnetic fields** — DC (static, "cusp"), AC, and traveling magnetic fields (TMF) — used industrially to control oxygen and dopant transport, melt-crystal interface shape, and defect formation in silicon boules up to 200–300 mm in diameter. Representative works include:

- Mühlbauer, A.; Muiznieks, A.; Virbulis, J.; Lüdge, A.; Riemann, H., *Journal of Crystal Growth*, 151 (1995), 66 (needle-eye FZ interface shape, heat transfer and fluid flow — see §3.3)
- Wetzel, Th.; Muiznieks, A.; Mühlbauer, A.; Gelfgat, Y.; Gorbunov, L.; Virbulis, J.; Tomzig, E.; von Ammon, W., "Numerical model of turbulent CZ melt flow in the presence of AC and CUSP magnetic fields and its verification in a laboratory facility," *Journal of Crystal Growth*, 205 (1999), 71–91.
- Virbulis, J.; Wetzel, Th.; Muiznieks, A.; Hanna, B.; Dornberger, E.; Tomzig, E.; Mühlbauer, A.; von Ammon, W., "Numerical investigation of silicon melt flow in large diameter CZ-crystal growth under the influence of steady and dynamic magnetic fields," *Journal of Crystal Growth*, 230 (2001), 92–99.
- Muižnieks, A.; Raming, G.; Mühlbauer, A.; Virbulis, J.; Hanna, B.; von Ammon, W., *Journal of Crystal Growth*, 230 (2001), 305.
- Mühlbauer, A.; Muiznieks, A.; Ratnieks, G.; Krauze, A.; Raming, G.; Wetzel, T., "Mathematical modelling of the industrial growth of large silicon crystals by CZ and FZ process," *COMPEL: The International Journal for Computation and Mathematics in Electrical and Electronic Engineering*, 22(1) (2003), 158–169.
- Ratnieks, G.; Muižnieks, A.; Buligins, L.; Raming, G.; Mühlbauer, A., *Magnetohydrodynamics*, 35 (1999), 223.
- Krauze, A.; Muiznieks, A.; Mühlbauer, A.; Wetzel, Th.; Gorbunov, L.; Pedchenko, A., "Numerical 2D modeling of turbulent melt flow in CZ system with AC magnetic fields," *Proc. Int. Sc. Colloquium Modelling for Electromagnetic Processing*, Hannover, 2003.
- Krauze, A.; Muiznieks, A.; Gorbunov, L.; Pedcenko, A., "Numerical modeling of 2D axisymmetric flow under the influence of DC EM field for industrial CZ silicon single crystal growth facilities" (2005).
- Krauze, A.; Jekabsons, N.; Muiznieks, A.; Lacis, U., "Applicability of LES turbulence modeling for CZ silicon crystal growth systems with traveling magnetic field," *Journal of Crystal Growth*, 312 (2010), 3225–3229.
- Muiznieks, A.; Krauze, A.; Nacke, B., "Convection phenomena in large melts including magnetic fields," *Journal of Crystal Growth*, 303 (2007), 211–220.

He also worked on **electro-vortical and travelling-magnetic-field flows** for solar-grade silicon purification by electron-beam melting:

- Krauze, A.; Muižnieks, A.; Bergfelds, K.; ... Chikvaidze, G., "Reduction of silicon crust on the crucible walls in silicon melt purifying processes with electron beam technology by low-frequency travelling magnetic fields," *Magnetohydrodynamics* (2011).

### 3.3 Floating Zone (FZ) silicon crystal growth: his most extensive body of work

Muižnieks's largest and most sustained research program concerned the **Floating Zone (FZ) technique**, the crucible-free method used to grow the highest-purity silicon crystals via high-frequency (HF) induction-heated "needle-eye" inductors, in long-standing partnership with the Leibniz Institute for Crystal Growth (IKZ Berlin, via Anke Lüdge and Helge Riemann). His FZ contributions span:

- **Molten-zone shape and interface calculation** under coupled 2D/3D HF electromagnetic fields:
  - Mühlbauer, A.; Muiznieks, A.; Virbulis, J.; Lüdge, A.; Riemann, H., "Interface shape, heat-transfer and fluid-flow in the floating-zone growth of large silicon-crystals with the needle-eye technique," *Journal of Crystal Growth*, 151 (1995), 66.
  - Ratnieks, G.; Muižnieks, A.; Mühlbauer, A., "Modelling of phase boundaries for large industrial FZ silicon crystal growth with the needle-eye technique," *Journal of Crystal Growth*, 255 (2003), 227–240.
  - Muižnieks, A.; Lacis, K.; Rudevičs, A.; ... Plate, M., "Development of numerical calculation of electromagnetic fields in FZ silicon crystal growth process," *Magnetohydrodynamics* (2010) — 2D axisymmetric to full 3D EM field calculation for molten-zone shape.
- **Transient behavior and process control of the molten zone**, forming the basis of the program **FZone**:
  - Rudevičs, A.; Muižnieks, A.; Ratnieks, G.; Mühlbauer, A.; Wetzel, T., "Numerical study of transient behaviour of molten zone during industrial FZ process for large silicon crystal growth," *Journal of Crystal Growth*, 266 (2004), 54–59.
  - Rudevics, A.; Muiznieks, A.; Riemann, H.; Luedge, A.; Ratnieks, G.; von Ammon, W., "Numerical study and comparisons with experimental data for transient behaviour of phase boundaries during industrial FZ process for silicon crystal growth," *Journal of Crystal Growth*, 275 (2005), e561–e565.
  - Dadzis, K.; Muižnieks, A.; Rudevičs, A.; Ratnieks, G., *Magnetohydrodynamics*, 41 (2005), 105.
- **Melt hydrodynamics, dopant segregation, and OpenFOAM-based 3D modeling** in FZ, developed with his students in the final years of his career:
  - Lacis, K.; Muižnieks, A.; Rudevičs, A.; Sabanskis, A., "Influence of DC and AC magnetic fields on melt motion in FZ large Si crystal growth," *Magnetohydrodynamics* (2010).
  - Lacis, K.; Muižnieks, A.; Jekabsons, N.; ... Nacke, B., "Unsteady 3D and analytical analysis of segregation process in floating zone silicon single crystal growth," *Magnetohydrodynamics* (2009).
  - Rudevics, A.; Lacis, K.; Muiznieks, A.; ... Nacke, B., "Using of open source code library OpenFOAM for 3D magnetohydrodynamic calculations in semiconductor crystal growth technologies" (2008).
  - Surovovs, K.; Muiznieks, A.; Sabanskis, A.; Virbulis, J., "Hydrodynamical aspects of the floating zone silicon crystal growth process," *Journal of Crystal Growth* (2014, posthumous) — 3D dopant transport modeling using program FZone coupled to a specialized OpenFOAM-based solver (FZSiFOAM).
- **Crystal surface morphology and molecular-dynamics-informed models**, his final line of inquiry:
  - Barinovs, G.; Sabanskis, A.; Muiznieks, A., "Study of silicon crystal surface formation based on molecular dynamics simulation results," *Journal of Crystal Growth* (2014, posthumous) — Wulff-construction analysis of crystal facets combining molecular dynamics (LAMMPS) results with growth-angle diagrams.
  - Barinovs, G.; Sabanskis, A.; Muiznieks, A., "Numerical study of silicon crystal ridge growth," *Journal of Crystal Growth* (2014, posthumous) — modeling of ridge-like surface protrusions on ⟨100⟩ dislocation-free silicon crystals related to {111} facet formation at the triple phase line.

### 3.4 Transient Czochralski process control (final research phase, completed posthumously)

In his last active period, Muižnieks and his group at the University of Latvia (notably Andrejs Sabanskis, K. Bergfelds, A. Krauze) extended their transient FZ methodology to the **Czochralski process with PID-based diameter and heater-power control**, culminating in the program **CZ-Trans**:

- Sabanskis, A.; Bergfelds, K.; Muiznieks, A.; Schröck, Th.; Krauze, A., "Crystal shape 2D modeling for transient CZ silicon crystal growth," *Journal of Crystal Growth*, 377 (2013), 9–16.

This paper — submitted while he was alive (received 11 February 2013), revised after his death (27 April 2013), and published with the author note "**Deceased**" appended to his name — presents a fully transient axisymmetric finite-element model of crystal–melt, melt–gas, and crystal–gas interface evolution during CZ start-cone ("shouldering") growth, using automatically remeshed unstructured finite elements to resolve the triple-phase-line region with high precision. It represents the capstone of his career-long effort to build physically grounded, computationally efficient, industrially relevant models of melt-crystal phase-boundary dynamics.

### 3.5 Physics education research (a parallel, smaller strand)

Alongside his crystal-growth work, Muižnieks maintained an active interest in **experimental and computational physics pedagogy**, publishing accessible quantitative-physics demonstrations suitable for classroom use:

- Muiznieks, A.; Dudareva, I., "Quantitative analysis of the damping of magnet oscillations by eddy currents in aluminum foil," *American Journal of Physics*, 80(9) (2012), 804–809 — a simplified mathematical model (verified experimentally) of eddy-current damping of a swinging permanent magnet above aluminum foil, using a magnetic-field sensor for quantitative measurement.
- Paulins, P.; Krauze, A.; Ozolinsh, M.; Muiznieks, A., "Warming of water in a glass," *Physics Education* (2016, posthumous) — an experimental and CFD study of convective warming of water from 0 °C, including determination of the temperature of maximum density, designed as a school-accessible demonstration.

### 3.6 The *Handbook of Crystal Growth* chapter — his definitive scientific legacy text

Muižnieks's most cited and most widely used single work is the comprehensive review chapter co-authored with his long-term collaborators, finalized and published after his death:

> **A. Muižnieks, J. Virbulis, A. Lüdge, H. Riemann, N. Werner**, "**Floating Zone Growth of Silicon**," in P. Rudolph (ed.), *Handbook of Crystal Growth*, 2nd edition, Vol. II: *Bulk Crystal Growth*, Elsevier, Boston, 2015, pp. 241–279 (ISBN 978-0-444-63303-3).

This chapter is the standard reference synthesis of FZ silicon growth physics and engineering: it covers the transition of FZ from a crucible-free purification technique to a high-purity single-crystal growth method, the needle-eye induction-coil technology and its diameter limitations (electric breakdown at the coil slit; thermomechanical stress at large diameters, then ~200 mm), the emerging granular-feedstock "gFZ" concept, and model-based predictive process control — closing with an outline of the detailed multiphysical numerical modeling (EM, thermal, hydrodynamic, segregation) required to advance the technology. In the publisher's contributor list, Muižnieks's affiliation is given as "Department of Physics, University of Latvia, Riga, Latvia (**Deceased 5 April 2013**)."

## 7. Summary of Scientific Contribution

Andris Muižnieks's career-long contribution was the construction of a physically rigorous, numerically tractable, and industrially validated modeling chain for **melt crystal growth of silicon**, spanning:

1. **Electromagnetic field calculation** in induction heating/melting systems (BEM/edge-FEM methods for HF fields).
2. **Coupled EM–hydrodynamic–thermal modeling** of turbulent melt convection under static and dynamic magnetic fields in Czochralski growth.
3. **Interface-shape and process modeling of the Floating Zone technique**, including molten-zone shape, dopant segregation, and transient process control, largely through the self-developed program suite **FZone**.
4. **Transient, PID-controlled Czochralski crystal-shape modeling** with unstructured, adaptively remeshed finite elements resolving the triple-phase-line region (program **CZ-Trans**).
5. **Crystal-surface and facet formation modeling**, connecting molecular-dynamics-derived surface energetics to macroscopic growth-angle and ridge-formation phenomena.
6. A standard-reference synthesis of the field in the *Handbook of Crystal Growth* (2015).

Through his University of Latvia group and his sustained partnership with Leibniz Universität Hannover, IKZ Berlin, and the Siltronic/Wacker industrial group, he helped establish Riga as a recognized international center for crystal-growth process modeling, a role his group (Virbulis, Sabanskis, and successors) has continued after his death.
