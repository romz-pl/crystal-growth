# Bibliography: Modeling & Simulation of Crystal Growth — Wilfried von Ammon (Siltronic / Wacker Siemens)

This bibliography compiles publications co-authored by Wilfried von Ammon (Siltronic AG,
formerly Wacker Siltronic / Wacker Siemens) that specifically concern the **modeling and
numerical simulation** of crystal growth processes — primarily Czochralski (CZ) silicon
growth, including melt convection, heat transfer, point-defect dynamics, oxygen transport,
magnetic-field effects, and thermal-stress modeling. Experimental-only or purely
device-physics papers are excluded unless simulation/modeling is a central component.

Entries are sorted chronologically by publication year.

### Notes on sourcing and completeness

This list was compiled from citation indices, review-article reference lists (Annual Reviews,
*physica status solidi*, *Central European Journal of Physics*), publisher abstract/search
pages, and conference proceedings abstracts. Wilfried von Ammon's modeling-relevant output is
concentrated in the period **1995–2015**, reflecting his role as head of R&D/CTO at Wacker
Siltronic overseeing the global CZ silicon growth simulation program developed jointly with
UCLouvain (F. Dupret group), University of Latvia (Muiznieks/Virbulis group), STR Inc.
(Makarov/Kalaev), and University of Pennsylvania (Sinno/Brown group). Some entries have
incomplete page ranges where the original source snippet did not provide them; these should be
verified against the primary journal record (e.g., via Crossref/DOI lookup) before formal
citation.
