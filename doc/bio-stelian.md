## 1. Overview

Carmen Stelian is a Romanian-French physicist specializing in the numerical modeling of crystal growth processes, with a career spanning academic research in Romania and applied/industrial modeling research in France. ## 5. Scientific Contributions and Research Themes

Carmen Stelian's published output — numbering upward of 55–60 peer-reviewed articles and conference contributions — is unified by the use of finite-element and finite-volume numerical modeling (notably via **COMSOL Multiphysics**) to solve coupled heat transfer, melt convection, species transport, and thermoelastic stress problems in industrially relevant crystal growth systems.
