## Notes on affiliation trajectory (as reflected in the bibliography)

- **2015–2020 (Kyushu University, RIAM, with K. Kakimoto):** core methodological period — development of transient/global heat-and-mass-transport models for Czochralski silicon melting and pulling, with emphasis on carbon and oxygen contamination chemistry (refs. 1–7).
- **~2021–2022 (transition period, associated with Kakimoto's move to Tohoku University and with the Usami group, IMR, Tohoku University):** application of global modeling and ML-surrogate methods to multicrystalline silicon defect formation and unsteady-growth prediction (refs. 8–11).
- **2025 (Nagoya University, Institute of Materials and Systems for Sustainability, with T. Ujihara):** extension of transient global modeling methodology from Czochralski silicon to top-seeded solution growth of SiC, including dynamic solid–liquid interface tracking under switching-flow conditions (ref. 12).
