# ENSEMBLE3's Scientific Advisory Board: A Technical and Scientific Report

## 1. Institutional Context

ENSEMBLE3 (formally: *Centre of ExcelleNce for nanophotonicS, advancEd Materials and novel crystal growth-Based technoLogiEs*) is a Polish research centre of excellence located in Warsaw, established under the European Union's Horizon 2020 "Teaming for Excellence" scheme (Grant Agreement No. 857543) in conjunction with the International Research Agenda (MAB) programme of the Foundation for Polish Science, co-financed by the European Regional Development Fund. The project runs from September 2019 to September 2026 with an overall budget on the order of €15 million.

The Centre was formed jointly by four "advanced" and "scientific" partner institutions from four countries:

- **Łukasiewicz Research Network – Institute of Electronic Materials Technology** (Poland, host/coordinating institution)
- **University of Warsaw**, Faculty of Chemistry (Poland)
- **Karlsruhe Institute of Technology** (Germany)
- **Sapienza University of Rome** (Italy)
- **CIC nanoGUNE – Nanoscience Cooperative Research Center** (Spain)

ENSEMBLE3's scientific mission builds explicitly on the legacy of crystal-growth pioneer Prof. Jan Czochralski, combining crystal-growth methodology with modern nanophotonics concepts — metamaterials, plasmonic materials, and materials with engineered electromagnetic properties — for applications spanning photonics, optoelectronics, telecommunications, solar-energy conversion, medicine, and aerospace. The project is coordinated by **Prof. Dorota A. Pawlak** (Institute of Electronic Materials Technology / University of Warsaw), who heads the Centre's Functional Materials Department.

## 2. Governance Structure and the Role of the Advisory Board

ENSEMBLE3's organizational structure includes **two distinct external advisory bodies**, consistent with the standard "Teaming for Excellence" governance model that Horizon 2020 imposed on centres of excellence of this type:

1. **International Scientific Committee (ISC)** — the Centre's Scientific Advisory Board proper, providing high-level scientific guidance, quality assurance, and strategic scientific direction.
2. **Consultative Innovation Council (CIC)** — an industry- and innovation-facing advisory body focused on commercialization, exploitation of results, and links with end users.

Per the Centre's own project reporting, these two boards convene periodically (in joint or coordinated meetings) to be updated on the Company's scientific performance and development plans and to provide independent, high-level feedback intended to be fed back into the optimization of the project's results and increased value for end users.

This report focuses on the **International Scientific Committee (ISC)**, which functions as ENSEMBLE3's Scientific Advisory Board in the conventional sense: an externally constituted panel of internationally distinguished researchers, independent of the Centre's own staff, whose role is to evaluate scientific direction, quality, and impact rather than to manage day-to-day operations.

### 3.3 Structural Observations

Several patterns in the ISC's composition are notable from a governance-design standpoint:

- **Institutional mirroring of the consortium**: each of ENSEMBLE3's four "advanced partner" institutions (KIT, Sapienza, nanoGUNE) plus the host institution (University of Warsaw) is directly represented on the ISC by at least one senior faculty member from that same institution. This is a common design in EU Teaming/Twinning projects, ensuring the advisory board retains institutional memory and continuity with the partnership agreement while nonetheless being able to offer arm's-length scientific critique.
- **Independent, non-consortium members**: the roster also includes senior figures with no formal institutional tie to the consortium — notably Cava (Princeton), Sandoghdar (MPI Science of Light), Pendry (Imperial College London), Braun (UIUC), and García de Abajo (ICFO) — who provide external, non-conflicted scientific perspective. Pendry, in particular, is one of the founding theorists of the field of metamaterials and transformation optics, giving the board direct access to foundational expertise in one of ENSEMBLE3's central research directions.
- **Disciplinary balance**: the ISC spans the full pipeline of ENSEMBLE3's declared research scope — crystal growth and structural chemistry (Woźniak, Cava), theoretical/foundational metamaterials and plasmonics physics (Pendry, Pitarke, García de Abajo), experimental nano-optics and near-field characterization (Sandoghdar, Hillenbrand, Li Voti), and applied optoelectronic device engineering (Lemmer, Wegener, Sibilia, Braun).
- **Geographic/institutional footprint**: members are drawn from Poland, Germany, Italy, Spain, the UK, and the US, reflecting both the consortium's European base and an intent to benchmark the Centre against globally leading (including US) research groups.

## 4. Function and Significance of the ISC within ENSEMBLE3's Governance

Within the "Teaming for Excellence" model used by Horizon 2020 and the Foundation for Polish Science's MAB programme, an external scientific advisory board of this kind typically serves several functions, consistent with how ENSEMBLE3 describes its own governance:

- **Scientific quality assurance**: periodic review of the Centre's scientific output, research strategy, and standing relative to international benchmarks in nanophotonics, plasmonics, and crystal-growth-based materials science.
- **Strategic guidance**: advice to the Centre's coordinator and management on research priorities, direction of new research groups (ENSEMBLE3 maintains several research groups, e.g., Functional Materials Technology, Optical Nanocharacterization, Inverse Materials Design, Next-Generation Energy Systems, Biophotonic Applications, Solar Energy Conversion, Oxide Single Crystals, and A3B5 Compound Semiconductors groups), and hiring of senior researchers/group leaders.
- **International visibility and credibility**: association with figures such as Pendry, Cava, Sandoghdar, and García de Abajo lends the young Centre (founded 2019) a level of international scientific credibility that is difficult to establish independently within the timeframe of a single Horizon 2020 funding cycle, and helps position ENSEMBLE3 as a competitive partner for collaborations and grant applications.
- **Complementary innovation oversight**: the parallel Consultative Innovation Council addresses commercialization and industrial relevance, meaning the ISC's own remit stays focused on scientific — as opposed to commercial — excellence.
