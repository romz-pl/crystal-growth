# Bibliography — Crystal Growth Modeling & Simulation Work of Thierry Duffar (Université Grenoble Alpes, SIMaP)

This bibliography lists journal articles, conference papers, and books authored or co-authored by **Thierry Duffar** (SIMaP-EPM / Université Grenoble Alpes, CNRS, Grenoble INP) that concern **modeling, numerical simulation, or analytical theory** of crystal growth processes. Purely experimental papers without a modeling component have been excluded. Entries are sorted chronologically (earliest first). Where a full citation could not be independently verified beyond title/journal/year/co-authors found in secondary sources, this is noted.

---

## 1990s

- Duffar, T., Paret-Harter, I., Dusserre, P. (1990). *[Modeling work on Bridgman-type crystal growth]*. **Journal of Crystal Growth**, 100, 171–184.

- Garandet, J.P., Duffar, T., Favier, J.J. (1990). On the scaling analysis of the solute boundary layer in idealized growth configurations. **Journal of Crystal Growth**, 106, 437–444. (Analytical boundary-layer model for solute segregation, later foundational to Duffar's segregation modeling work.)

- Duffar, T., Dusserre, P., Abadie, J. (1997). Crucible de-wetting during Bridgman growth in microgravity. II. Smooth crucibles. **Journal of Crystal Growth**, 179, 397–409. (Analytical/theoretical model of the dewetting phenomenon.)

- Barat, C., Duffar, T., Dusserre, P., Garandet, J.P. (1998). Estimation of the curvature of the solid–liquid interface during Bridgman crystal growth. **Journal of Crystal Growth**. (Analytical/numerical model for interface curvature from thermal measurements.)

- Barat, C., Duffar, T., Dusserre, P., Garandet, J.P. (1999). Chemical segregation in vertical Bridgman growth of GaInSb alloys. **Crystal Research and Technology**, 34, 449–456. (Numerical simulation of segregation, validated against experiment.)

## 2000s

- Duffar, T., Dusserre, P., Picca, F., Lacroix, S., Giacometti, N. (2000). Bridgman growth without crucible contact using the dewetting phenomenon. **Journal of Crystal Growth**, 211, 434–440. (Includes theoretical/analytical modeling of the dewetting mechanism.)

- Duffar, T., Dusserre, P., Giacometti, N., Benz, K.W., Fiederle, M., Launay, J.C., Dieguez, E., Roosen, G. (2001). *[Modeling of detached/dewetted Bridgman growth under microgravity]*. **Acta Astronautica**, 48, 157–161.

- Duffar, T. (2004) (with L. Bizet). Contribution to the stability analysis of the dewetted Bridgman growth under microgravity conditions. **Crystal Research and Technology**, 39(6), 491–500. (Analytical stability model of the dewetted meniscus.)

- Duffar, T. (2005). Bulk crystal growth under microgravity conditions. In P. Capper (Ed.), *Bulk Crystal Growth of Electronic, Optical and Optoelectronic Materials*, Wiley, Chichester, pp. 477–524. (Review chapter with substantial modeling content on microgravity growth processes.)

- Duffar, T., Nadri, A. *[Analytical/numerical modeling of twinning in semiconductor crystal growth — precursor work later formalized in Duffar & Nadri (2010)]*.

- Braescu, L., Duffar, T. (2008). *[Numerical/analytical modeling of interface shape and stress]*. **Journal of Crystal Growth**, 310, 484.

- Mangelinck-Noël, N., Duffar, T. (2008). Modelling of the transition from a planar faceted front to equiaxed growth: Application to photovoltaic polycrystalline silicon. **Journal of Crystal Growth**, 311, 20–25. (Numerical grain-structure model based on triple-phase-line crystallography.)

- Epure, S., Duffar, T., Braescu, L. (2008). Comparison between analytical and numeric determination of the interface curvature during dewetted Bridgman crystal growth. **Journal of Crystal Growth**. (Direct analytical-vs-numerical modeling comparison.)

- Sylla, L., Paulin, J.P., Vian, G., Garnier, C., Duffar, T. (2008). Effect of residual impurities on the dewetting of antimonide melts in fused silica crucibles in the case of bulk crystal growth. **Materials Science and Engineering: A**, 495, 208–214. (Includes modeling of dewetting thermodynamics/kinetics.)

## 2010s

- **Duffar, T. (Ed.) (2010).** *Crystal Growth Processes Based on Capillarity: Czochralski, Floating Zone, Shaping and Crucible Techniques.* Wiley-Blackwell, Hoboken. ISBN 978-0-470-71244-3. (Edited volume; Duffar authored/co-authored chapters covering capillarity theory, Czochralski process dynamics and control, Verneuil and shape-stability modeling, and Vertical Bridgman/dewetting modeling — see next entry.)

- Duffar, T., Sylla, L. (2010). Vertical Bridgman and dewetting. In T. Duffar (Ed.), *Crystal Growth Processes Based on Capillarity*, Wiley-Blackwell, Hoboken, pp. 355–408. (Modeling chapter on dewetted Bridgman growth.)

- Epure, S., Duffar, T., Braescu, L. (2010). On the capillary stability of the crystal-crucible gap during dewetted Bridgman process. **Journal of Crystal Growth**, 312, 1416–1420. (Analytical capillary-stability model.)

- Duffar, T., Nadri, A. (2010). On the twinning occurrence in bulk semiconductor crystal growth. **Scripta Materialia**, 62, 955–960. (Theoretical/physical model of twin formation at the growth interface.)

- Beaudhuin, M., Duffar, T., Lemiti, M., Zaidat, K. (2011). One-dimensional model of the equiaxed grain formation in multi-crystalline silicon. **Journal of Crystal Growth**, 319, 106–113. (Analytical 1D nucleation/growth model.)

- Duffar, T., Nadri, A. (2013). The grain–grain–liquid triple phase line during solidification of multi-crystalline silicon. **Comptes Rendus Physique**, 14, 185–191. (Theoretical crystallographic/kinetic model.)

- Duffar, T., et al. (co-author) (2014). Chemical Segregation of Titanium in Sapphire Single Crystals Grown by Micro-Pulling-Down Technique: Analytical Model and Experiments. **Crystal Growth & Design**, 14(12), 6492–6496. (Analytical 1D segregation model for μ-PD growth.)

- Stelian, C., Duffar, T. (2017). Modeling Interface Shape in Czochralski Growth of Sapphire Crystals. **Crystal Research and Technology**, 52, 1700176. (Deformable-mesh 2D-axisymmetric numerical simulation of radiative heat transfer and melt-crystal interface shape.)

- Stelian, C., Nehari, A., Lebbou, K., Duffar, T. (2017). Drop model for continuous milli-beads production. **Crystal Research and Technology**, 52, 1700037. (Experimental, theoretical, and numerical analysis of drop formation dynamics.)

- Stelian, C., Duffar, T., et al. (2018/2019). Numerical Analysis of Thermal Stress in Semi-Transparent Oxide Crystals Grown by Czochralski and EFG Methods. **Crystal Research and Technology**. (3D numerical modeling of thermal stress in sapphire and langatate crystals.)

- Stelian, C., Velázquez, M., Veber, P., et al., Duffar, T. (2019). Experimental and numerical investigations of the Czochralski growth of Li2MoO4 crystals for heat-scintillation cryogenic bolometers. **Journal of Crystal Growth**. (Numerical modeling of temperature field, melt convection, and thermo-elastic stress.)

## 2020s

- Duffar, T. (2020). Use of Growth-Rate/Temperature-Gradient Charts for Defect Engineering in Crystal Growth from the Melt. **Crystals**, 10(10), 909. (Analytical modeling framework — growth-rate/temperature-gradient charts — with application to twinning and equiaxed-growth models for photovoltaic silicon.)

- Cherif, M., Duffar, T., Carroz, L., et al. (2020). On the growth and structure of Al2O3-Y3Al5O12-ZrO2:Y solidified eutectic. **Journal of the European Ceramic Society**. (Includes coupled-growth modeling analysis of the ternary eutectic.)

- Xuan, L., Duffar, T. (2021). Physico-Chemical Model for Dopant Charge State Conversion During Ti:sapphire Crystal Growth. **Journal of Crystal Growth**. (Original physico-chemical/diffusion-reaction model with numerical simulation in COMSOL Multiphysics.)

- Ahmine, A., Velázquez, M., Nagirnyi, V., et al., Duffar, T. (2021). Conventional Czochralski growth of large Li2MoO4 single crystals. **Journal of Crystal Growth**. (Furnace configuration optimized by numerical simulation of heat and mass transfer.)

- Xuan, L., Duffar, T. (2022). On the way to modelling dopant valence in ionic crystal growth: the Ti:Al2O3 case. **CrystEngComm**. (Modeling framework for dopant valence conversion in ionic crystals.)

- Xuan, L., Pisch, A., Duffar, T. (2022). Thermodynamic Calculations of Ti Ion Concentrations and Segregation Coefficients during Ti:Sapphire Crystal Growth. **Crystal Growth & Design**, 22(4), 2407–2416. (Thermodynamic/segregation-coefficient modeling combined with numerical simulation of annealing.)

- Ahmine, A., Djemia, P., Fivel, M.C., et al., Duffar, T. (2022). Mechanical properties of Li2MoO4 single crystals. **Journal of Applied Physics**. (DFT calculations of elastic constants combined with experimental characterization.)

- Muzy, J., Fivel, M.C., Labor, S., et al., Duffar, T. (2023). Influence of growth process and crystal defects on sapphire brittleness. **Journal of Crystal Growth**. (Includes mechanical/fracture modeling linked to growth-induced defects.)

- Xuan, L., Duffar, T. (2025). The growth of titanium doped sapphire for laser application. **Progress in Crystal Growth and Characterization of Materials**, 71(2), 100666. (Review including numerical simulation of thermo-elastic stress and internal-radiation heat transfer models for Ti:sapphire growth.)
