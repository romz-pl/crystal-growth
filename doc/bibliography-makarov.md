# Bibliography: Modeling and Simulation of Crystal Growth — Yuri N. Makarov and the STR Group

This bibliography compiles the modeling- and simulation-focused publications of Yuri N. Makarov, spanning his early work in Leningrad/St. Petersburg (Ioffe Institute, LETI) through the founding and operation of Soft-Impact Ltd. and STR Group (Semiconductor Technology Research, Inc. / STR-Group / STR Systems / IH STR), and his later affiliations (Nitride Crystals Inc.). Only papers, proceedings, book chapters, and technical reports concerned with **numerical/physical modeling and simulation** of crystal growth (melt, vapor/sublimation, and solution methods) and directly associated transport phenomena are included. Purely experimental papers without a modeling component are excluded, as are device/epitaxy-only papers unrelated to bulk or melt crystal growth simulation.

Entries are listed chronologically by publication year. Author lists are reproduced as found in the source record; where only partial author strings were recoverable, this is noted.
