# William R. Wilcox — Crystal Growth Modeling & Simulation Bibliography

**Affiliation note:** William R. Wilcox (Distinguished University Professor Emeritus) spent his crystal-growth career at **Clarkson University** (Department of Chemical Engineering; founding director of the New York State Center for Advanced Materials Processing and of NASA's Consortium for Commercial Crystal Growth in Space), not "Clark University." This bibliography is compiled under his correct affiliation, Clarkson University. Earlier work was conducted at the University of Southern California, TRW Semiconductors, and The Aerospace Corporation.

**Scope:** This list is restricted to Wilcox's publications that involve **modeling, numerical simulation, or quantitative transport-theory analysis** of crystal growth (mass transfer, heat transfer, convection, interface shape, capillarity/detached solidification, stability theory). Purely experimental papers, general reviews without modeling content, and non-crystal-growth work are excluded. This is a strong, citation-trail-built list, not confirmed exhaustive — some conference proceedings and technical reports may be missing.

## Chronological Bibliography

- Wilcox, W.R. (1969). *Validity of the stagnant film approximation for mass transfer in crystal growth and dissolution*. Materials Research Bulletin, 4, 265–274.

- Chang, C.E., Wilcox, W.R. (1974). *Analysis of surface tension driven flow in floating zone melting*. International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer, 19, 355–366.

- Chang, C.E., Wilcox, W.R. (1974). *Control of interface shape in the vertical Bridgman–Stockbarger technique*. Journal of Crystal Growth, 21, 135–140.

- Chang, C.E., Wilcox, W.R. (1975). *Inhomogeneities due to thermocapillary flow in floating zone melting*. Journal of Crystal Growth, 28, 8–12.

- Sen, S., Wilcox, W.R. (1975). *Influence of crucible on interface shape, position and sensitivity in the vertical Bridgman–Stockbarger technique*. Journal of Crystal Growth, 28, 26–40.

- Chang, C.E., Wilcox, W.R. (1976). *Analysis of heat flow in generalized crystal growth systems by the moving heat source method*. International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer, 19, 1201–1207.

- Yip, V.F.S., Wilcox, W.R. (1976). *Influence of thermocapillary flow on interface shape in encapsulated crystal growth*. Journal of Crystal Growth, 36, 29–35.

- Wilcox, W.R. (1976). *A generalized treatment of mass transfer in crystal growth*, in Wilcox, W.R. (ed.), *Preparation and Properties of Solid State Materials*, Vol. 2, Dekker, New York.

- Clark, P.A., Wilcox, W.R. (1980). *Influence of gravity on thermocapillary convection in floating zone melting of silicon*. Journal of Crystal Growth, 50, 461–469.

- Fu, T.W., Wilcox, W.R. (1980). *Influence of insulation on stability of interface shape and position in the vertical Bridgman–Stockbarger technique*. Journal of Crystal Growth, 48, 416–424.

- Fu, T.W., Wilcox, W.R. (1981). *Influence of a shield on interface shape and position in the vertical Bridgman–Stockbarger technique*. Journal of Crystal Growth, 51, 557–567.

- Fu, T.W., Wilcox, W.R. (1982). *Convective and interfacial instabilities during vertical Bridgman–Stockbarger crystal growth*. Journal of Crystal Growth, 57, 91–102.

- Chen, P.-S., Wilcox, W.R., Shlichta, P.J. (1979). *Free convection about a rectangular prismatic crystal growing from a solution*. International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer, 22, 1669–1681. *(analytical/numerical convection model for solution-growth geometry)*

- Wilcox, W.R., Chang, C.E., Shlichta, P.J., Chen, P.S., Kim, C.K. (1980s NASA technical reports). *Computer calculations of transport processes in floating-zone melting and solution crystal growth in a microgravity environment* — NASA-sponsored modeling studies supporting Spacelab crystal-growth experiment design.

- Wilcox, W.R. (1983). *Influence of convection on the growth of crystals from solution*. Journal of Crystal Growth, 65, 133–142.

- Wilcox, W.R. (1989). *Fundamentals of Solution Growth*, in Arend, H., Hulliger, J. (eds.), *Crystal Growth in Science and Technology*, NATO ASI Series B, Vol. 210, Springer, Boston, MA, pp. 217–238. *(includes transport/convection modeling treatment of solution growth systems)*

- Wilcox, W.R. (1993). *Transport phenomena in crystal growth from solution*. Progress in Crystal Growth and Characterization of Materials, 26, 153–194. *(review with quantitative transport modeling, critiquing the stagnant-film model)*

- Wilcox, W.R. (1994). *Handbook of Crystal Growth, Vol. 1: Fundamentals* (book review contribution), Acta Crystallographica.

- Regel, L.L., Wilcox, W.R. (1990s). *Detached Solidification in Microgravity: A Review*. International Center for Gravity Materials Science and Applications, Clarkson University — analytical/correlative modeling of the detachment criterion (correlating equation for predicting detached vs. attached growth modes).

- Regel, L.L., Wilcox, W.R. (2001). *Processing by Centrifugation*. Springer, Heidelberg — includes modeling of convective/capillary effects under enhanced gravity for crystal growth.

- Larson, D.J. Jr., Wilcox, W.R., Zhang, H., et al. (2002–2003). *Integrated numerical model for detached solidification*, combining a global heat-transfer sub-model (heat/mass transfer, melt convection, macrosegregation, interface tracking via multizone adaptive grid) with a meniscus/triple-phase-line wall-contact sub-model — presented at the 2002 Microgravity Materials Science Conference (NASA document ID 20030060551).

- Duval, W.M.B., Wilcox, W.R. (and collaborators, NASA-funded, 2000s). Numerical/control-oriented modeling studies of Bridgman furnace thermal design and detached-growth stability, feeding into subsequent detached-Bridgman capillary-stability modeling literature (cited by Yeckel & Derby and by Balint & Epure).

- Wilcox, W.R. (2008). *Crystal Growth Technology: From Fundamentals and Simulation to Large-Scale Production* (review). Crystal Growth & Design, 8(11), 4190.

## Notes on Coverage

- Wilcox's core modeling contributions cluster in three periods: (1) **1969–1983**, foundational stagnant-film and moving-heat-source transport models plus the Chang/Sen/Fu/Clark vertical-Bridgman and floating-zone interface-shape and thermocapillary-convection modeling series; (2) **1989–1994**, solution-growth transport-phenomena modeling and critique of simplified boundary-layer models; (3) **1990s–2000s**, detached-solidification (dewetted Bridgman/VGF) modeling in collaboration with L.L. Regel, W.M.B. Duval, D.J. Larson, and H. Zhang, much of it NASA-microgravity-funded.
- Wilcox's analytical correlating-equation work on detached solidification (with Regel) underlies much of the subsequent numerical dynamic-stability modeling literature by Yeckel & Derby (University of Minnesota) and Balint & Epure — those are *not* Wilcox's own papers and are excluded here except where cited for context.
- Several 1980s NASA/Aerospace Corporation technical reports on floating-zone and solution-growth transport modeling are known to exist but could not be independently confirmed with full citation data from open sources; they are noted qualitatively above rather than listed as discrete numbered entries.
