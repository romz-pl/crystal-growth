## Notes on scope and completeness

- This list draws on ResearchGate's author profile (43 total publications indexed, cited-by count 739), Web of Science / ACS citation trails, ScienceDirect special-issue metadata, and cross-citations in third-party reviews (e.g., Fujiwara et al., *High Temperature Materials and Processes*, 2022).
- Vizman's modeling output falls into several recognizable programs: (1) Czochralski/EMCZ melt convection and magnetic-field control (Erlangen collaboration with G. Müller and J. Friedrich, ~1999–2007); (2) directional solidification of multicrystalline silicon for photovoltaics, including traveling/rotating/cusp magnetic fields and mechanical stirring (2011–2023, partly with Fraunhofer IISB/K. Dadzis and CEA/J.-P. Garandet); (3) earlier Bridgman/EFG semi-transparent crystal thermal-stress and interface modeling (1996–2000, with I. Nicoara); (4) coupled growth-and-spectroscopy studies of doped fluoride crystals (BaF₂, CaF₂) grown by Bridgman-Stockbarger, where numerical simulation of the growth process accompanies optical characterization (2024–2026).
- A small number of early/grey-literature items (conference proceedings, Annals of West University of Timişoara articles, and pre-2000 items co-authored during his Erlangen research stay) may exist beyond what standard indices surface; if a fully exhaustive archival sweep (e.g., of IWMCG proceedings volumes 1989–2022 or the Romanian Physical Society archives) is wanted, that would require targeted institutional-repository access beyond open web search.
