# Bibliography of Crystal Growth Modeling and Simulation Publications by Nikolay G. Ivanov (Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University)

This bibliography collects the crystal-growth-related **modeling and simulation** publications co-authored by **Nikolay G. Ivanov** (Higher School of Applied Mathematics and Computational Physics / Fluid Dynamics Research Laboratory, Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University, formerly St. Petersburg State Polytechnical University). Entries are restricted to work on numerical/computational modeling of melt convection, heat transfer, and related transport phenomena in bulk crystal growth (chiefly Czochralski silicon growth); Ivanov's separate, much larger body of work on building/room ventilation, indoor airflow, and unrelated CFD topics is excluded. Sorted chronologically by publication year.

## 2020s (later reference works citing/building on Ivanov's Cz melt-convection modeling)

- The RANS/LES hybridization method for Czochralski melt convection introduced by **N.G. Ivanov** et al. (2003) continues to be cited and used as a baseline turbulence-modeling reference in subsequent Czochralski crystal-growth simulation literature, e.g.:
  - D.V. Borisov, V.V. Kalaev, "ILES of melt turbulent convection with conjugated heat transfer in the crucible and gas flow for Czochralski silicon crystal growth system," *Journal of Crystal Growth*, Vol. 573 (2021), 126289.
  - Kalaev et al., "Extended Hypothesis for Reynolds Stress Tensor and Turbulent Heat Flux Modeling Within a Novel k–ε Model for Prediction of Crystal Growth from the Melt," *Crystal Research and Technology* (2024).
  - "A modified hypothesis of Reynolds stress tensor modeling for mixed turbulent convection in crystal growth," *Journal of Crystal Growth*, Vol. 578 (2022), 126423 (cites N.G. Ivanov et al. 2003 among core turbulence-model references for Cz melt flow).

### Notes on scope and completeness

- Ivanov's crystal-growth modeling output is concentrated in the period **2001–2004**, produced jointly with the St. Petersburg State Polytechnical University aerodynamics/CFD group (E.M. Smirnov, A.G. Abramov, A.B. Korsakov, D.K. Zaitsev) in collaboration with the Soft-Impact/STR group (V.V. Kalaev, Yu.N. Makarov, I.Yu. Evstratov, A.I. Zhmakin) and Wacker Siltronic (E. Dornberger, J. Virbulis, E. Tomzig, W. von Ammon). All entries concern turbulent melt convection, conjugate heat transfer, and magnetic-field (MHD) effects during Czochralski silicon crystal growth.
- From the mid-2000s onward, Ivanov's own publication record (confirmed via his institutional and ResearchGate profiles) shifts almost entirely to unrelated CFD applications — building/room ventilation, indoor airflow, thermal comfort, spacecraft cabin air distribution, and free-convection boundary-layer studies — none of which concern crystal growth.
- This list is believed to be a **strong, near-exhaustive account of Ivanov's crystal-growth-specific modeling papers**, cross-validated against multiple independent citation trails (industrial crystal-growth simulation reviews, competing groups' reference lists, and Ivanov's own publication profile), but it is not guaranteed complete — a small number of conference proceedings or Russian-language items from this period may not be indexed in the sources consulted.
