# Bibliography: Crystal Growth Modeling and Simulation Work by Matthias Bickermann (IKZ Berlin)

This bibliography compiles the modeling- and simulation-focused publications co-authored by **Prof. Dr. Matthias Bickermann**, Deputy Director and Department Head "Volume Crystals" at the Leibniz-Institut für Kristallzüchtung (IKZ) Berlin, and Professor for "Fundamentals and Methods in Crystal Growth" at TU Berlin.

## Scope and methodology

Bickermann's body of work (~150 peer-reviewed publications since 1998) is predominantly experimental, centered on physical vapor transport (PVT) growth of AlN and SiC bulk crystals, melt growth of transparent semiconducting oxides, and defect/optical characterization. Only a small subset of his publications are properly classed as **modeling or simulation** work — i.e., papers whose central contribution is a theoretical, numerical, or computational model of a crystal growth process (heat transfer, mass transport, growth kinetics, interface dynamics), as opposed to purely experimental growth/characterization studies. This bibliography includes only entries meeting that criterion, drawn from his complete publication record.

Entries confirmed as authored by other IKZ research groups that work on numerical crystal growth models (e.g., the IKZ "Model Experiments" / NEMOCRYS group led by K. Dadzis, involving A. Enders-Seidlitz, A. Wintzer, J. Pal, S. Foroushani, B.E. Abali) were checked but **excluded**, as Bickermann does not appear as a co-author on their publications, despite institutional proximity.

Sorted chronologically (oldest to newest).

## Notes on excluded borderline entries

- **Numerical Modelling of the Czochralski Growth of β-Ga2O3** (W. Miller, K. Böttcher, Z. Galazka, J. Schreuer, *Crystals* 7 [1] (2017) 26) — a genuine IKZ-affiliated numerical modeling paper on the growth of a material system central to Bickermann's oxide-crystal program, but Bickermann is **not a co-author**; excluded per the author-focused scope of this bibliography.
- Publications by the IKZ "Model Experiments"/NEMOCRYS group (K. Dadzis, A. Enders-Seidlitz, A. Wintzer, J. Pal, S. Foroushani, B. E. Abali, I. Tsiapkinis) on open-source Czochralski multiphysics simulation (Elmer, OpenFOAM, FEniCS/dolfinx) — active and substantial modeling work at IKZ, and Arved Wintzer's PhD thesis in this area was supervised in part by Bickermann's academic environment at TU Berlin, but no direct Bickermann co-authorship was found on the underlying journal articles; excluded on the same basis.
- Numerous digital X-ray imaging / in-situ growth visualization papers (e.g., Wellmann et al. 1999, 2000) were reviewed but excluded as primarily experimental/diagnostic rather than modeling contributions, notwithstanding some interpretive use of heat-transfer arguments.
