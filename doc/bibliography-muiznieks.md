# Andris Muiznieks — Bibliography of Crystal Growth Modeling and Simulation Publications

**Compiled:** August 2026
**Scope:** Modeling / numerical simulation publications on melt crystal growth (Czochralski and Floating Zone silicon, primarily) authored or co-authored by **Andris Muiznieks**, University of Latvia (Faculty of Physics and Mathematics / Institute of Solid State Physics / Institute of Numerical Modelling), and Institute of Electrothermal Process Engineering, Leibniz Universität Hannover. Sorted chronologically by publication year.

> **Note on completeness:** This list is compiled from citation-trail cross-referencing (ResearchGate author page, Google-Scholar-indexed citing works, journal reference lists, and the University of Latvia's own faculty archive) rather than from a single authoritative CV or publication list, since no complete self-maintained bibliography by Muiznieks was located. It should be regarded as a **strong, near-exhaustive list**, not a confirmed-complete one. Andris Muiznieks passed away in **April 2013**; several 2013–2015 entries are posthumous publications of work completed with his direct involvement (his co-authors explicitly mark him "Deceased" in at least one 2013 paper).

## Notes on Scope and Related Works

- Muiznieks's crystal-growth modeling work is essentially split between two intertwined process families: **Floating Zone (FZ) silicon growth** (needle-eye technique, high-frequency electromagnetic heating, molten-zone shape, dopant segregation, thermal stress) carried out largely at the Institute of Electrothermal Process Engineering, Leibniz Universität Hannover, in long-standing collaboration with the Leibniz-Institut für Kristallzüchtung (Anke Lüdge, Helge Riemann) and Wacker/Siltronic (industrial CZ partners); and **Czochralski (CZ) silicon growth** (turbulent melt convection, magnetic-field control — DC, AC/traveling, CUSP fields — dislocation generation, transient crystal-shape/interface modeling), carried out at the University of Latvia in collaboration with Wacker Siltronic AG.
- Several entries above are proceedings papers from the *International Workshop on Modeling in Crystal Growth* series and the *International Scientific Colloquium: Modelling for Electromagnetic Processing / Material Processing* (Hannover/Riga), reflecting the applied, industrially oriented character of this body of work.
- Co-authors whose own bibliographies substantially overlap with and extend Muiznieks's work (already covered as separate entries in this reference library) include Andrejs Sabanskis, Janis Virbulis, Armands/A. Krauze, Kaspars Lacis, and the FZ-focused Leibniz-Institut für Kristallzüchtung group (Anke Lüdge, Helge Riemann).
