# Juris (Janis) Virbulis — Bibliography of Crystal Growth Modeling and Simulation Publications

**Scope note:** Janis (Juris) Virbulis worked at Wacker Siemens/Wacker Siltronic (Burghausen, Germany) during the 1990s–early 2000s, then moved to the Institute of Numerical Modelling (formerly Laboratory for Mathematical Modelling of Environmental and Technological Processes), University of Latvia, Riga, where he now heads the Laboratory of Semiconductor Technologies. His publication name appears as both "J. Virbulis" and "Janis Virbulis" in the literature. Entries below are restricted to modeling/simulation work on crystal growth (Czochralski, floating zone, pedestal method), including closely related process-modeling papers (electron-beam heating systems, sintering of growth-related crucibles). Non-crystal-growth modeling work (hydrogeology, indoor air quality/CFD, COVID-19 aerosol risk, etc.) is excluded as out of scope. Entries are sorted chronologically by publication year; within a year, order follows publication sequence where determinable.

### Notes on completeness

- This list is a **strong, near-exhaustive compilation** built primarily from ResearchGate's publication listing (97 total publications for J. Virbulis, of which the crystal-growth-modeling subset above has been extracted) plus citation-trail cross-referencing (Handbook of Crystal Growth chapter references, MDPI *Crystals* reference lists, ACS Omega review references, and Annual Reviews chapter references). It is **not confirmed complete** — several 1998–2000 and pre-1995 Wacker/Siemens-era conference proceedings (e.g., early ANSYS-based FZ interface-shape work with A. Muiznieks and A. Mühlbauer) may exist in venues not indexed by the sources consulted, and a handful of 2004–2011 Magnetohydrodynamics/conference entries co-authored with K. Lācis, A. Rudevičs, and G. Ratnieks (on segregation and 3D EM modeling) that cite Virbulis as a collaborator rather than co-author were excluded pending direct authorship confirmation.
- Excluded from this bibliography (out of crystal-growth-modeling scope): Virbulis's substantial body of work on Baltic Artesian Basin hydrogeological modeling, Riga flood-risk modeling, building heat-transfer/indoor-air-quality CFD, and COVID-19 aerosol infection-risk modeling.
