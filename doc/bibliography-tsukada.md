# Bibliography: Crystal Growth Modeling and Simulation — Taketoshi (Takao) Tsukada (Tohoku University)

This bibliography compiles the modeling- and simulation-focused publications of **Takao Tsukada** (塚田隆夫), Institute of Multidisciplinary Research for Advanced Materials, Tohoku University (formerly Kyushu University). Entries are restricted to works concerning the numerical/theoretical modeling and simulation of crystal growth processes — global furnace heat-transfer models, melt convection, radiative transport, thermal-stress analysis, and related transport-phenomena modeling for Czochralski, floating-zone, EFG, hydrothermal (ammonothermal), traveling liquidus-zone (TLZ), and electromagnetic-levitation crystal/droplet growth systems. Purely experimental measurement papers (e.g., thermophysical property measurement without a significant simulation component) and papers unrelated to crystal growth (e.g., food engineering, polymer films, solvent extraction) are excluded, except where a paper couples measurement with an explicit numerical/simulation model of a crystal-growth-relevant process.

Sorted chronologically by publication year.

### Notes on scope and completeness

- This list is built primarily from Tsukada's own laboratory publication list (Tohoku University Process and Chemical Engineering Laboratory, che.tohoku.ac.jp/~pcel) cross-checked against citation trails in third-party crystal-growth-modeling literature (e.g., reviews of CZ oxide growth, GaN ammonothermal growth simulation reviews, radiative heat transfer literature).
- Excluded from this list: pure thermophysical-property measurement papers without an explicit crystal-growth process model (e.g., EML calorimetry/emissivity/thermal-conductivity measurement papers for Cu, Cu–Co, Cu–Fe alloys, ionic liquids), polymer/nanoparticle thin-film papers, food engineering papers, drop/bubble electrohydrodynamics papers not tied to a crystal growth furnace, and solvent-extraction papers — none of which concern crystal growth modeling.
- Two book chapters with substantial modeling/simulation content are included; several other Japanese-language book chapters and review (解説・総説) articles from the lab's list are omitted here as they largely summarize (rather than present new) modeling work, except where noted.
- Name note: Tsukada publishes in English-language journals as **Takao Tsukada**; "Taketoshi Tsukada" and "Takao Tsukada" refer to the same Tohoku University researcher (Institute of Multidisciplinary Research for Advanced Materials).
- This bibliography is believed to be near-exhaustive for Tsukada's core Czochralski/floating-zone/hydrothermal/TLZ crystal-growth modeling output, built from the laboratory's own publication list, but has not been independently verified against Scopus/Web of Science for 100% completeness.
