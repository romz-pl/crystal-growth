# Bibliography: Nobuyuki Imaishi (Kyushu University) — Crystal Growth Modeling & Simulation

Chronological listing of modeling/numerical-simulation publications by Nobuyuki Imaishi (Kyushu University, Institute for Materials Chemistry and Engineering) and close collaborators, covering Czochralski global furnace analysis, oxide melt convection, thermocapillary/Marangoni instability, floating-zone/liquid-bridge modeling, and related melt-growth transport phenomena. Experimental-only and non-modeling papers are excluded.

---

## 1988

- Tsukada, T., Imaishi, N., Hozawa, M. (1988). *[Numerical analysis of interface shape and melt convection in Czochralski crystal growth]*. Cited as foundational cause–effect study on melt convection and interface inversion in later Imaishi survey papers.

## 1994

- Tsukada, T., Hozawa, M., Imaishi, N. (1994). Numerical study of interface inversion and melt convection effects in Czochralski growth. (Cited in Imaishi's 2005 CZ oxide survey as part of the interface-inversion literature developed by the Tohoku/Kyushu group.)

## 1995

- Tsukada, T., Kakinoki, K., Hozawa, M., Imaishi, N. (1995). Effect of internal radiation within crystal and melt on Czochralski crystal growth of oxide. *International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer*, 38(17), 2939–2951. — Global heat-transfer analysis for inductively heated CZ furnace, incorporating internal (semitransparent) radiation in crystal and melt; shows melt/crystal interface shape strongly affected by optical absorption coefficients.

## 1996 (approx.) — Radiation shield study

- Tsukada, T., Imaishi, N., Hozawa, M., Fujinawa, K. Effect of a radiation shield on silicon CZ growth. *Journal of Crystal Growth* (Tohoku/Kyushu collaboration). — Finite-element study of radiation-shield insertion effects on melt/crystal temperature profile and interface shape in silicon Czochralski growth.

## 1997

- Tsukada, T., Kakinoki, K., Hozawa, M., Imaishi, N., Shimamura, K., Fukuda, T. (1997). Numerical and experimental studies on crack formation in LiNbO₃ single crystal. *Journal of Crystal Growth*, 180(3–4), 543–550. — Global heat-transfer analysis of an inductively heated CZ furnace coupled with 3-D thermal-stress calculation in the crystal; furnace-design modifications (after-heater insertion) validated numerically and experimentally to avoid crystal cracking.

## 1999

- Jing, C. J., Imaishi, N., Yasuhiro, S., Miyazawa, Y. (1999). Three-dimensional numerical simulation of spoke pattern in oxide melt. *Journal of Crystal Growth*, 200(3–4), 204–212. — Early 3-D simulation of Marangoni-driven "spoke pattern" convective instability on an oxide melt free surface in CZ configuration.

## 2000

- Jing, C. J., Imaishi, N., Sato, T., Miyazawa, Y. (2000). Three-dimensional numerical simulation of oxide melt flow in Czochralski configuration. *Journal of Crystal Growth*, 216(1–4), 372–388.
- Jing, C. J., Imaishi, N., Yasuhiro, S., Sato, T., Miyazawa, Y. (2000). Three-dimensional numerical simulation of spoke pattern in oxide melt (extended study). *Journal of Crystal Growth* (companion 3-D study; further characterization of the spoke instability regime).

## 2001

- Imaishi, N., Yasuhiro, S., Akiyama, Y., Yoda, S. (2001). Numerical simulation of oscillatory Marangoni flow in half-zone liquid bridge of low Prandtl number fluid. *Journal of Crystal Growth*, 230(1–2), 164–171. — Foundational half-zone liquid-bridge simulation for low-Pr fluid (silicon-relevant), widely cited baseline for later floating-zone thermocapillary-instability studies.
- Lappa, M., Yasuhiro, S., Imaishi, N. (2001). 3-D numerical simulation of on-ground Marangoni flow instabilities in liquid bridges of low Prandtl number fluid. *International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer* (collaborative extension of half-zone modeling to 3-D instability structure under terrestrial gravity).

## 2002

- Li, M., Li, Y., Imaishi, N., Tsukada, T. (2002). Global simulation of a silicon Czochralski furnace. *Journal of Crystal Growth*, 234(1), 32–46. — Finite-element global (multi-domain) analysis coupling momentum, heat and mass transfer for a small silicon CZ furnace, including melt/crystal, melt/silica and melt/gas interface equilibria and unity segregation coefficient.
- Li, Y.-R., Akiyama, Y., Imaishi, N., Tsukada, T. (2002). Global analysis of a small Czochralski furnace with rotating crystal and crucible. *Journal of Crystal Growth*, 255(1–2), 81–92. — Global finite-element simulations across crystal-rotation (0–20 rpm) and crucible counter-rotation (0 to −2.5 rpm) ranges; evaluates thermocapillary effects on oxygen transport and Vs,n/Gn ratio.

## 2003

- Li, Y.-R., Ruan, D.-F., Imaishi, N., Wu, S.-Y., Peng, L., Zeng, D.-L. (2003). Global simulation of a silicon Czochralski furnace in an axial magnetic field. *International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer*, 46(15), 2887–2897. — Extends global CZ furnace model to axial magnetic-field control (0–0.3 T) of melt flow/oxygen transport with rotating crystal and crucible.
- Yasuhiro, S., Imaishi, N. (2003). Numerical simulation of oscillatory axisymmetric Marangoni convection in small liquid bridge. *Kyushu University Institutional Repository* report/paper — stability diagram for critical Marangoni number vs. aspect ratio (1.0–3.0), Pr = 3.34, adiabatic surface; compared against energy-method predictions.
- Jing, C. J., Kobayashi, M., Tsukada, T., Hozawa, M., Fukuda, T., Imaishi, N., Shimamura, K., Ichinose, N. (2003). Effect of RF coil position on spoke pattern on oxide melt surface in Czochralski crystal growth. *Journal of Crystal Growth*, 259(4), 367–376.
- Nakajima, K., Mizoguchi, S., Yasuhiro, S., Imaishi, N. (2003). Marangoni convection driven by thermocapillary and solutocapillary forces in the Fe–O alloy pool during the solidification or remelting process. *Metallurgical and Materials Transactions B*, 34(1), 37–49. — Combined experimental/numerical study of thermo-solutal Marangoni convection in a liquid Fe–O alloy pool.

## 2004

- Li, Y.-R., Imaishi, N., Azami, T., Hibiya, T. (2004). Three-dimensional oscillatory flow in a thin annular pool of silicon melt. *Journal of Crystal Growth*, 260(1–2), 28–42. — Unsteady 3-D simulations of thermocapillary and thermocapillary-buoyancy flow in shallow annular silicon-melt pools (CZ-relevant free-surface geometry); identifies spoke-pattern and hydrothermal-wave oscillatory regimes with critical Marangoni number determination.
- Li, Y.-R., Imaishi, N., Akiyama, Y., Peng, L., Wu, S.-Y., Tsukada, T. (2004). Effect of temperature coefficient of surface tension on oxygen transport in a small CZ furnace. *Journal of Crystal Growth*, 266(1–3), 48–53. — Global simulation study isolating the influence of dσ/dT on melt flow pattern, temperature field and oxygen-transport distribution.
- Hayashi, A., Kobayashi, M., Jing, C. J., Tsukada, T., Hozawa, M. (2004). Numerical simulation of the Czochralski growth process of oxide crystals with a relatively thin optical thickness. *International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer*, 47(24), 5287–5294. (Kyushu/Tohoku collaborative group work directly building on the Imaishi/Tsukada global CZ-oxide radiation modeling line.)

## 2005

- Li, Y.-R., Quan, X.-J., Peng, L., Imaishi, N., Wu, S.-Y., Zeng, D.-L. (2005). Three-dimensional thermocapillary-buoyancy flow in a shallow molten silicon pool with Cz configuration. *International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer*, 48(15), 3199–3210. — Unsteady 3-D simulation (depth = 3 mm) showing a two-step 2-D→3-D→oscillatory-3-D flow transition as radial ΔT increases; determines critical conditions for instability onset; performed by Y.-R. Li as visiting professor under N. Imaishi at Kyushu University.
- Imaishi, N., Shimamura, K., Ichinose, N., et al. (2005) [context paper cited within Imaishi CZ-oxide survey]. — Related transparent-oxide-melt convection study establishing the reduced-temperature-gradient/absent-spoke regime in highly transparent oxide melts.
- **Imaishi, N.** (2005). Numerical simulation of CZ crystal growth of oxide. *Progress survey article* (Kyushu University). — Review/survey of the author's numerical studies on CZ oxide crystal growth: global inductively-heated-furnace heat-transfer model with internal radiation in crystal/melt, effect of optical properties on interface shape, and characterization of convective (Marangoni/baroclinic-wave) instabilities including a flow-regime diagram.

## 2006

- Li, Y.-R., Yu, C.-J., Wu, S.-Y., Peng, L., Imaishi, N. (2006). Global simulation of a Czochralski furnace for silicon crystal growth against the assumed thermophysical properties. *Crystal Research and Technology*, 41(7), 636–644. — Sensitivity study of global CZ furnace simulation results (heater power, interface deflection, axial temperature gradient, oxygen concentration) to assumed melt thermophysical property values; shows axial magnetic-field application is comparatively insensitive to this uncertainty.

## 2009

- Shi, W., Imaishi, N. (2009). [Determination of critical conditions for onset of oscillatory flows in shallow annular pool of silicone oil, Pr = 6.7]. Cited in later annular-pool stability literature as the Shi & Imaishi baseline stability result.
- Ermakov, M. K., Imaishi, N. (2009). Influence of buoyancy force on thermocapillary convection instability in the differentially heated annular pools of silicon melt. *Microgravity Science and Technology*, 21(S1), 61–65. — Numerical stability analysis of combined thermocapillary-buoyancy convection in annular silicon-melt pools (Ri = 20 mm, Ro = 40 mm, d = 1–10 mm); comparison of 3-D simulation with linear stability analysis (LSA).

## 2010

- Shi, W., Ermakov, M. K., Imaishi, N. (2010). Effect of pool rotation on thermocapillary convection in shallow annular pool of silicone oil. *Journal of Crystal Growth* / related issue — numerical simulation + LSA of pool-rotation effects (Ta = 0, 0.322, 0.806) on hydrothermal-wave critical Marangoni number and azimuthal wavenumber; silicon-melt CZ-configuration companion study of rotation-driven flow-pattern transitions (ω up to 2000) via LSA with Hopf-bifurcation identification.
- Ermakov, M. K., Imaishi, N. (2010). Stability of thermocapillary convection in rotating shallow annular pool of silicon melt. *Microgravity Science and Technology*, 22(3), 355–361. — Linear stability analysis of rotating shallow annular silicon-melt pools (Pr = 0.011) under microgravity, Ta = 0–1513.9; shows pool rotation stabilizes steady axisymmetric thermocapillary flow and derives critical azimuthal wavenumber trends.
- Shi, W., Li, G., Liu, X., Li, Y.-R., Peng, L., Imaishi, N. (2010). Thermocapillary convection and buoyant-thermocapillary convection in the annular pools of silicon melt and silicone oil. *Journal of Superconductivity and Novel Magnetism*, 23(7), 1169–1172.

## 2018

- Yano, T., Nishino, K., Matsumoto, S., Ueno, I., Komiya, A., Kamotani, Y., Imaishi, N. (2018). Report on microgravity experiments of dynamic surface deformation effects on Marangoni instability in high-Prandtl-number liquid bridges. *Microgravity Science and Technology*, 30(5), 599–610. — Combined experimental/modeling analysis of free-surface deformation effects on Marangoni instability onset in high-Pr half-zone liquid bridges (JAXA microgravity campaign).

## 2023

- [Collaborative acknowledgement paper] Dependency of transition in thermocapillary convection on volume ratio in annular pools of large-Pr fluid in microgravity. *International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer* (2023). — Later-generation annular-pool thermocapillary instability study explicitly built on, and acknowledging guidance from, N. Imaishi's methodology (critical-condition determination via 3-D simulation, following the Shi & Imaishi line of work).

## Notes on scope and completeness

- This list prioritizes papers where **Imaishi is a named author** and the work is explicitly **numerical/modeling** (global furnace analysis, CFD/FEM melt-convection simulation, linear stability analysis, thermal-stress simulation). Purely experimental papers (e.g., crystal-pulling experiments without an accompanying simulation component) are excluded even when co-authored by Imaishi.
- Several entries from the mid-1990s and early 2000s (marked with bracketed context) are reconstructed from citation lists in later survey articles (notably the 2005 "Numerical Simulation of CZ Crystal Growth of Oxide" survey) where the original primary-source bibliographic record (exact volume/page) could not be independently confirmed; these are flagged for verification against a citation index (Scopus/Web of Science) before formal use.
- Imaishi's research group produced two major modeling threads: (1) **global CZ furnace analysis** (with T. Tsukada, M. Hozawa, and later Y.-R. Li/Chongqing University) covering silicon and oxide melts, radiative heat transfer, magnetic-field control, and thermal stress; and (2) **thermocapillary/Marangoni instability in liquid bridges and annular pools** (with S. Yasuhiro, M. K. Ermakov, W. Shi), extending into floating-zone and microgravity liquid-bridge configurations that continued to be cited and extended by other groups (Osaka University, Ryukyus, Chongqing) through the 2020s.
- A definitive exhaustive list would require direct access to Imaishi's Scopus Author ID or the Kyushu University Pure portal profile page, which returned no directly indexable results in this session; the listing above was constructed by cross-referencing citation trails across ~15 independent search queries covering CZ oxide growth, silicon CZ global simulation, magnetic-field control, thermal stress, and floating-zone/liquid-bridge/annular-pool instability literature.
