# Alexander I. Zhmakin — Bibliography of Crystal Growth Modeling and Simulation Publications

**Subject:** Alexander I. Zhmakin, Ioffe Institute (A.F. Ioffe Physical-Technical Institute), St. Petersburg, Russia; also affiliated with Softimpact Ltd. / STR Group.

**Scope:** This bibliography is restricted to publications concerning the **numerical modeling and simulation** of crystal growth processes (vapour-, melt-, and solution-phase bulk crystal growth, and closely related epitaxial growth transport phenomena). Zhmakin's broader body of work — on non-Fourier heat conduction, cryobiology, bioheat transfer, LED device modeling, shock-wave gas dynamics, MHD flows, and molecular dynamics of nanostructures — is excluded except where a paper's core content is transport modeling directly supporting a crystal growth process. Papers on the fundamental low-Mach-number/hyposonic flow methods that Zhmakin himself later applied to vapour-phase epitaxial and CVD growth reactors are included, since they are foundational to his crystal-growth modeling programme; pure shock-wave/MHD gas-dynamics papers unconnected to growth reactors are excluded.

Sorted chronologically by publication date.

## Notes on Completeness

This list was compiled from Zhmakin's ResearchGate publication record (146 total publications across all subject areas) cross-referenced against citation trails in his own 2014/2015 review papers and the "Industrial Challenges for Numerical Simulation of Crystal Growth" paper (with D.Kh. Ofengeim). It should be regarded as a **strong, near-exhaustive** list for the crystal-growth-modeling portion of his output, built from an author-level bibliographic search rather than a systematic database export (e.g., Scopus/Web of Science), and is not guaranteed complete — in particular for:
- Russian-language journal articles and conference proceedings from the 1990s that may not be indexed on ResearchGate;
- internal Softimpact Ltd. / STR Group technical reports and code theory manuals (e.g., the *Virtual Reactor: Sublimation Bulk Crystal Growth Simulator* theory manual) not formally published as citable articles;
- some conference proceedings papers that exist in near-duplicate journal and proceedings versions, both of which are listed here where independently identifiable.

Two Zhmakin authors appear in the co-author lists above: **A.I. Zhmakin** (the subject of this bibliography, Ioffe Institute) and **I.A. Zhmakin** (a distinct, related researcher who co-authored SiC dislocation/thermoelastic-strain modeling work with A.I. Zhmakin); papers co-authored by both are noted accordingly.
