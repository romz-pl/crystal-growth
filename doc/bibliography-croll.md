# Arne Cröll (University of Freiburg) — Bibliography of Crystal Growth Modeling and Simulation Work

This bibliography collects publications by Arne Cröll (Crystallography, Institute of Geo- and
Environmental Natural Sciences, University of Freiburg) that involve numerical modeling, computational
simulation, or theoretical/analytical modeling of crystal growth processes. Purely experimental papers
are excluded unless they include a significant numerical/theoretical modeling component (e.g. numerical
stability analysis, finite-element/finite-volume simulation, phase-field simulation, dynamic particle-engulfment
modeling). Entries are sorted chronologically by publication year.

---

## 1996

- Kaiser, T., Cröll, A., Dold, P., Benz, K.W. (1996). *Numerical simulation of heat and mass transfer in
  silicon crystal growth by the floating zone method*. Proc. 2nd European Symposium on Fluids in Space,
  p. 245.

## 1998

- Kaiser, T., Benz, K.W. [with Cröll, A. as co-investigator on the underlying experimental/numerical
  series] (1998). *Floating-zone growth of silicon in magnetic fields. III. Numerical simulation*.
  Journal of Crystal Growth, 183, 564–572.
  — Numerical simulation (temperature distributions, velocity fields, dopant concentration/segregation,
  and stability analysis for critical Marangoni numbers) directly paired with Cröll's experimental
  Parts I and II in the same series (J. Cryst. Growth 183, 545–553 and 554–563, 1998).

## 1999

- Cröll, A., Dold, P., Kaiser, T., Szofran, F.R., Benz, K.W. (1999). *The influence of static and
  rotating magnetic fields on heat and mass transfer in silicon floating zones*. Journal of the
  Electrochemical Society, 146, 2270–2275.
  — Combines experiment with numerical/theoretical treatment of magnetically damped convective heat and
  mass transfer.

## 2001

- Dold, P., Cröll, A., Lichtensteiger, M., Kaiser, T., Benz, K.W. (2001). *Floating zone growth of
  silicon in magnetic fields: IV. Rotating magnetic fields*. Journal of Crystal Growth, 231, 95–106.
  — Includes numerical modeling of rotating-magnetic-field-driven flow alongside experimental results.

## 2002

- Cröll, A., Kaiser, N., Cobb, S.D., Szofran, F.R., Volz, M.P. et al. (2002). *Contact angles and
  surface tension measurements* [detached/dewetted growth series]. Journal of Crystal Growth, 242, 45.
  — Analytical/theoretical modeling of meniscus and contact-angle behavior underlying detached
  (dewetted) Bridgman growth.

## 2009

- Cröll, A., Volz, M.P. (2009). *Detached Bridgman Growth—A Standard Crystal Growth Method with a New
  Twist*. MRS Bulletin, 34, 245–250.
  — Review article by Cröll synthesizing the theoretical/numerical modeling literature (capillary
  stability, meniscus mechanics) underpinning detached solidification.

## 2016

- Aufgebauer, H., Kundin, J., Emmerich, H., Azizi, M., Reimann, C., Friedrich, J., Jauß, T.,
  Sorgenfrei, T., **Cröll, A.** (2016). *Phase-field simulations of particle capture during the
  directional solidification of silicon*. Journal of Crystal Growth, 446, 12–21.
  — Phase-field numerical simulation of SiC particle pushing/engulfment at a moving solidification
  front.

## 2017

- Derby, J.J., Tao, Y., Reimann, C., Friedrich, J., Jauß, T., Sorgenfrei, T., **Cröll, A.** (2017).
  *A quantitative model with new scaling for silicon carbide particle engulfment during silicon crystal
  growth*. Journal of Crystal Growth, 463, 100–109.
  — Rigorous finite-element numerical model (Galerkin FEM, arbitrary Lagrangian–Eulerian mesh) of
  particle-induced interface deflection and Gibbs–Thomson effects during SiC particle engulfment;
  co-authored by Cröll, who supplied the experimental/microgravity data validated against the model.

- Reimann, C., Trempa, M., Friedrich, J., **Cröll, A.**, et al. (2017). *Particle engulfment dynamics
  under oscillating crystal growth conditions*. Journal of Crystal Growth, 468, 24–33.
  — Numerical/dynamic modeling of the pushing–engulfment transition under time-varying (oscillating)
  growth-rate conditions, extending the Derby–Cröll SiC engulfment modeling program.

### Notes on scope and completeness

- Arne Cröll's overall publication record (100+ papers, per academic indices) is overwhelmingly
  **experimental**, centered on microgravity crystal growth (floating zone, Bridgman/detached
  solidification, Czochralski) of Si, Ge, GeSi, GaSb, GaAs, InSb, and related semiconductors. The
  entries above are the subset in which Cröll is a named author/co-author on work whose core
  contribution is numerical, computational, or mathematical/analytical modeling and simulation (as
  opposed to experimental measurement or purely descriptive/review content).
- Several closely related simulation papers by other groups (e.g. Th. Kaiser's independent numerical
  work, Yutao Tao and Jeffrey Derby's broader particle-engulfment modeling program at the University of
  Minnesota) cite and build on Cröll's experimental data extensively but were excluded here because
  Cröll is not a listed co-author on those specific papers.
- Given the fragmentary indexing of 1990s conference proceedings and German-language technical reports
  (e.g. DLR/BMBF *Schlussbericht* project reports on Si and compound-semiconductor microgravity growth),
  this list should be treated as a strong, well-evidenced partial bibliography rather than a
  guaranteed-exhaustive one. Institutional archives (University of Freiburg Crystallography group
  publication list, DLR project databases) may hold additional co-authored numerical/simulation studies
  not indexed in the general scientific literature search engines used to compile this list.
