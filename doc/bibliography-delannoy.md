# Yves Delannoy (SIMaP, Grenoble INP) — Bibliography of Crystal Growth Modeling & Simulation Publications

Chronological, exhaustive-as-possible bibliography of works by Yves Delannoy (SIMaP – Science et Ingénierie des Matériaux et Procédés, Grenoble INP / Université Grenoble Alpes, EPM group) restricted to publications concerning the **numerical modeling and simulation** of crystal growth, melt crystallization, directional solidification, and closely related transport-phenomena modeling that underpins his crystal growth work (electromagnetic stirring/MHD coupling, plasma/thermal modeling of purification feeding into crystallization, free-surface and solidification interface modeling).
