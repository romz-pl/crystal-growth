# Other major integrated substrate manufacturers

## Japan
+ Wafer Works Japan (compound semiconductor wafers)
+ Showa Denko Materials (specialized III–V materials)
+ Mitsubishi Chemical (electronic materials)

## China (China has become the largest producer of III–V substrates during the last decade.)
+ PAM-XIAMEN
+  Tongmei Xtal Technology 
+ [BeijingTongmei Xtal Technology (often abbreviated Tongmei)](./doc/house-tongmei.md)
+ TankeBlue Semiconductor (primarily sapphire but expanding into compound semiconductors)
+ Grinm Semiconductor Materials
+ Yunnan Germanium (Ge substrates)

## United States
+ Coherent Corp.
+ IQE plc (primarily epitaxial wafers rather than bulk substrates)
+ 5N Plus (Ge and III–V materials)

## Europe
+ Umicore (Ge substrates and specialty materials)
+ IQE plc (epiwafers)

## Taiwan / Korea
+ Wafer Works Corporation
+ SK Siltron (SiC, some compound semiconductor activities)
