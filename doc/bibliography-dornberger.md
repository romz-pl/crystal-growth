# Bibliography: Evgenii (Erich) Dornberger — Crystal Growth Modeling & Simulation

Scope note: this bibliography covers the modeling- and simulation-related publications of **Erich (Evgenii) Dornberger**, long associated with **Wacker Siltronic AG / Wacker Chemie AG** (Burghausen, Germany), on Czochralski silicon crystal growth: global heat/mass transfer simulation, melt convection modeling, oxygen transport modeling, and — his dominant later body of work — intrinsic point-defect dynamics modeling (vacancies, self-interstitials, OSF ring formation, void/COP formation, nitrogen-doping effects). Purely process/manufacturing items with no modeling or simulation content (e.g. polysilicon feedstock production papers, patents) are excluded per scope. Entries are sorted chronologically by publication date; where month/season is known it is given alongside the year.

---

1. **The dependence of bulk defects on the axial temperature gradient of silicon crystals during Czochralski growth**
   W. von Ammon, E. Dornberger, H. Oelkrug, H. Weidner
   *Journal of Crystal Growth*, June 1995.
   Correlates OSF-ring critical pull rate with axial temperature gradient computed for different heat-shield/hot-zone configurations — an early application of thermal simulation to defect prediction.

2. **The Dependence of Ring-Like Distributed Stacking Faults on the Axial Temperature Gradient of Growing Czochralski Silicon Crystals**
   E. Dornberger, W. von Ammon
   *Journal of The Electrochemical Society*, May 1996.
   Shows the radial position of the OSF ring can be predicted from the computed axial temperature gradient G at the melt/crystal interface.

3. **Transient computer simulation of a CZ crystal growth process**
   E. Dornberger, W. von Ammon, N. Van den Bogaert, F. Dupret
   *Journal of Crystal Growth*, September 1996.
   Time-dependent numerical simulation of heat transfer during CZ silicon growth, validated against industrial-furnace experiments.

4. **Simulation of grown-in voids in Czochralski silicon crystals**
   E. Dornberger, J. Esfandyari, D. Graf, G. Vanhellemont, U. Lambert, F. Dupret, et al.
   Book chapter / conference proceedings, 1997.
   Models grown-in voids as spherical vacancy clusters that nucleate homogeneously and grow during crystal cooling, using thermal history plus point-defect dynamics.

5. **On the dynamics of the oxidation-induced stacking-fault ring in as-grown Czochralski silicon crystals**
   T. Sinno, R. A. Brown, W. von Ammon, E. Dornberger
   *Applied Physics Letters*, April 1997.
   Predicts OSF ring behavior from point-defect dynamics simulation; fits preexponential constants for equilibrium point-defect concentrations and diffusivities to experimental data.

6. **Influence of boron concentration on the oxidation-induced stacking fault ring in Czochralski silicon crystals**
   E. Dornberger, D. Gräf, M. Suhren, P. Wagner, W. von Ammon
   *Journal of Crystal Growth*, October 1997.
   Extends the OSF-ring critical-value model, Ccrit(CB), to account for boron doping level via experiment and numerical simulation.

7. **Thermal simulation of the Czochralski silicon growth process by three different models and comparison with experimental results**
   E. Dornberger, E. Tomzig, A. Seidl, S. Schmitt, H.-J. Leister, Ch. Schmitt, G. Müller
   *Journal of Crystal Growth*, October 1997.
   A systematic comparative benchmark of three independent global heat-transfer simulation codes against thermocouple measurements in an industrial CZ silicon puller (EKZ 1300); an early, influential code-intercomparison/validation study in the field.

8. **Prediction of OSF Ring Dynamics and Grown-in Voids in Czochralski Silicon Crystals**
   E. Dornberger (advisors: F. Dupret, W. von Ammon)
   PhD Thesis, Université catholique de Louvain, November 1997.
   Dornberger's doctoral thesis: reviews the state of simulation in silicon crystal growth and develops coupled thermal/point-defect models for predicting OSF ring position and grown-in void distributions.

9. **Defects in As-Grown Czochralski Silicon: Measurement, Modeling and Simulation**
   J. Vanhellemont, E. Dornberger, D. Graef, J. Esfandyari, U. Lambert, R. Schmolke, W. von Ammon, P. Wagner
   Proceedings of the Kazusa Akademia Park Forum on the Science and Technology of Silicon Materials, Chiba, November 1997, pp. 173–196.
   Combined measurement/modeling/simulation review of as-grown defects in CZ silicon.

10. **Defects in AS-Grown Silicon and their Evolution During Heat Treatments**
    J. Vanhellemont, E. Dornberger, J. Esfandyari, D. Gräf, U. Lambert, P. Wagner, W. von Ammon
    *Materials Science Forum*, December 1997.
    Discusses void/oxide-precipitate formation mechanisms via simulation of point-defect aggregation during crystal cooling.

11. **Determination of intrinsic point defect properties in silicon by analyzing OSF ring dynamics and void formation**
    E. Dornberger, T. Sinno, J. Esfandyari, et al.
    Book chapter, 1998.
    Combines numerical simulation with experiment to extract diffusion coefficients and equilibrium concentrations of intrinsic point defects.

12. **Simulation of non-uniform grown-in void distributions in Czochralski silicon crystals**
    E. Dornberger, J. Esfandyari, J. Vanhellemont, et al.
    Book chapter, 1998.
    Models radially non-uniform grown-in void distributions arising from radially varying vacancy concentration profiles.

13. **Influence of Residual Point Defect Supersaturation on the Formation of Grown-In Oxide Precipitate Nuclei in CZ-Si**
    G. Kissinger, J. Vanhellemont, U. Lambert, D. Gräf, E. Dornberger, H. Richter, et al.
    *Journal of The Electrochemical Society*, May 1998.
    Uses a first-order approximation model, fed by growth-rate/defect-density data, to explain residual point-defect supersaturation effects on oxide precipitate nucleation.

14. **Curriculum vitae of oxide precipitates: From nucleation during crystal growth to their final destination in processed wafers**
    G. Kissinger, J. Vanhellemont, U. Lambert, E. Dornberger, H. Richter, et al.
    Book chapter, 1998.
    Traces oxide precipitate nucleation as a function of residual vacancy concentration and cooling rate, integrating point-defect modeling of the growth stage.

15. **Recent progress in understanding oxygen precipitation in silicon**
    J. Vanhellemont, J. Esfandyari, G. Obermeier, E. Dornberger, et al., G. Kissinger
    Book chapter, 1998.
    Review of quantitative oxygen-precipitation modeling in CZ silicon, incorporating point-defect and thermal-history simulation results.

16. **Oxygen distribution in silicon melt during a standard Czochralski process studied by sensor measurements and comparison to numerical simulation**
    A.-S. Mühe, R. Backofen, J. Fainberg, E. Dornberger (et al.), W. von Ammon
    *Journal of Crystal Growth*, March 1999.
    Compares electrochemical oxygen-sensor measurements in the melt to numerical simulation of oxygen transport, with and without crystal growth and at varying crucible rotation rates.

17. **Bulk properties of very large diameter silicon single crystals**
    W. von Ammon, E. Dornberger, P.O. Hansson
    *Journal of Crystal Growth*, March 1999.
    Applies Voronkov (V/G) theory to explain why larger-diameter CZ crystals are harder to grow defect-free, informing subsequent modeling efforts.

18. **Global model of oxygen transport during Czochralski growth of silicon crystals**
    Y. Makarov, I. Yu. Evstratov, V. Kalaev, E. Dornberger (et al.), W. von Ammon
    Conference paper, June 1999.
    Presents a global model coupling heat/mass transfer with oxygen transport simulation for CZ silicon.

19. **Challenges for economical growth of high quality 300 mm CZ Si crystals**
    E. Tomzig, W. von Ammon, E. Dornberger, W. Zulehner
    *Microelectronic Engineering*, July 1999.
    Discusses process/simulation challenges specific to scale-up from 200 mm to 300 mm CZ silicon crystal growth.

20. **Boron retarded self-interstitial diffusion in Czochralski growth of silicon crystals and its role in oxidation-induced stacking-fault ring dynamics**
    T. Sinno, H. Susanto, R. A. Brown, W. von Ammon, E. Dornberger
    *Applied Physics Letters*, September 1999.
    Comprehensive point-defect dynamics model incorporating boron–point-defect interactions (parameterized via tight-binding calculations) to explain OSF ring position dependence on boron doping.

21. **Defect engineering of Czochralski single-crystal silicon**
    T. Sinno, E. Dornberger, W. von Ammon, R. A. Brown, F. Dupret
    *Materials Science and Engineering: R: Reports*, July 2000.
    Major review article on native point-defect dynamics modeling as a tool for predicting and engineering microdefect distributions in CZ silicon; synthesizes the modeling framework developed across the group's prior decade of work.

22. **Numerical study of 3D unsteady melt convection during industrial-scale CZ Si-crystal growth**
    I. Yu. Evstratov, V. Kalaev, A. I. Zhmakin, E. Dornberger, J. Virbulis, W. von Ammon, et al.
    *Journal of Crystal Growth*, January/April 2002.
    3D hybrid RANS/Large-Eddy-Simulation model of turbulent melt convection in CZ silicon systems, verified against temperature measurements; includes magnetic-field suppression of turbulent fluctuations.

23. **The impact of nitrogen on the defect aggregation in silicon**
    W. von Ammon, R. Hölzl, J. Virbulis, E. Dornberger, R. Schmolke, D. Gräf
    *Journal of Crystal Growth*, June 2001 (Erratum April 2002).
    Investigates, via nitrogen-doped floating-zone crystal experiments and modeling, the suppression of vacancy/interstitial aggregation through the N₂+V mechanism.

24. **On the Mechanism of Defect Suppression in Nitrogen-Doped Silicon Single Crystals**
    W. von Ammon, R. Hölzl, J. Virbulis, E. Dornberger, D. Gräf
    *Solid State Phenomena*, November 2001.
    Companion analysis of nitrogen-doping defect-suppression mechanism, modeling density/size distribution of vacancy aggregates (voids/COP).

25. **Global model of Czochralski silicon growth to predict oxygen content and thermal fluctuations at the melt–crystal interface**
    I. Yu. Evstratov, V. Kalaev, V. N. Nabokov, E. Dornberger, J. Virbulis, W. von Ammon, et al.
    *Microelectronic Engineering*, May 2001.
    Couples axisymmetric quasi-steady global heat/mass transfer computation (radiative exchange, conduction) with Large Eddy Simulation of turbulent melt convection to predict oxygen incorporation and interface thermal fluctuations.

26. **Numerical investigation of silicon melt flow in large diameter CZ-crystal growth under the influence of steady and dynamic magnetic fields**
    J. Virbulis, Th. Wetzel, A. Muiznieks, E. Dornberger, W. von Ammon, et al.
    *Journal of Crystal Growth*, August 2001.
    2D axisymmetric turbulent melt-flow model examining alternating, steady, and combined magnetic field effects on large-diameter CZ silicon melt flow.

27. **Modeling analysis of unsteady three-dimensional turbulent melt flow during Czochralski growth of Si crystals**
    I. Yu. Evstratov, V. Kalaev, A. I. Zhmakin, E. Dornberger, W. von Ammon, et al.
    *Journal of Crystal Growth*, August 2001.
    3D unsteady Large-Eddy-Simulation-based model of turbulent CZ melt convection, validated with melt/crucible-surface temperature measurements; examines crucible rotation-rate effects on turbulent structures.

28. **Modeling of transient point defect dynamics in Czochralski silicon crystals**
    E. Dornberger, W. von Ammon, J. Virbulis, B. Hanna, T. Sinno
    *Journal of Crystal Growth*, August 2001.
    Simulates transient point-defect transport under abrupt pull-rate changes; reproduces experimentally observed interstitial-reservoir and defect-pattern formation via a hot-zone designed by simulation.

29. **Silicon crystals for future requirements of 300 mm wafers**
    E. Dornberger, J. Virbulis, B. Hanna, W. von Ammon, et al.
    *Journal of Crystal Growth*, July 2001.
    Discusses simulation-guided approaches for meeting the increased process-control demands of 300 mm CZ silicon crystal growth.

30. **Defects in Silicon Crystals and Their Impact on DRAM Device Characteristics**
    E. Dornberger, D. Temmler, W. von Ammon
    *Journal of The Electrochemical Society*, April 2002.
    Links octahedral vacancy-aggregate (COP) formation, informed by point-defect dynamics simulation, to device-relevant wafer characteristics.

31. **Numerical study of 3D unsteady melt convection during industrial-scale CZ Si-crystal growth** (extended/second version)
    I. Yu. Evstratov, V. Kalaev, A. I. Zhmakin, Janis Virbulis, et al.
    *Journal of Crystal Growth*, 2002.
    Extended treatment of the 3D RANS/LES hybrid melt-convection model for industrial-scale CZ Si growth.

32. **Analysis of magnetic field effect on 3D melt flow in CZ Si growth**
    N. G. Ivanov, A. B. Korsakov, E. M. Smirnov, E. Dornberger, W. von Ammon, et al.
    *Journal of Crystal Growth*, March 2003.
    Numerically simulates 3D turbulent melt convection under an axial stationary magnetic field using a RANS/LES hybridization with a differential turbulence model.

33. **Prediction of bulk defects in CZ Si crystals using 3D unsteady calculations of melt convection**
    V. Kalaev, D. Lukanin, V. Zabelin, E. Dornberger, J. Virbulis, W. von Ammon, et al.
    *Materials Science in Semiconductor Processing*, January 2003.
    Couples 3D unsteady melt-convection simulation (validated crystallization-front geometry) with point-defect incorporation analysis to predict bulk defect distributions.

34. **Calculation of bulk defects in CZ Si growth: impact of melt turbulent fluctuations**
    V. Kalaev, D. Lukanin, V. Zabelin, E. Dornberger, J. Virbulis, W. von Ammon, et al.
    *Journal of Crystal Growth*, March 2003.
    Extends the 3D unsteady melt-convection/defect-prediction framework to quantify the impact of turbulent fluctuations on bulk defect formation.

35. **Transient and quasi-stationary simulation of heat and mass transfer in Czochralski silicon crystal growth**
    A. Voigt, C. Weichmann, J. Nitschkowski, E. Dornberger, R. Hölz, et al.
    *Crystal Research and Technology*, June 2003.
    Couples transient/quasi-stationary simulation of heat and mass transfer to point-defect-ratio (V/G) analysis for predicting the prevailing defect species.

36. **Global Parameterization of Multiple Point-Defect Dynamics Models in Silicon**
    T. A. Frewen, T. Sinno, E. Dornberger, R. Hoelzl, W. von Ammon, H. Bracht
    *Journal of The Electrochemical Society*, November 2003.
    Regresses a globally robust, thermophysically consistent parameter set for point-defect properties in silicon by fitting simultaneously across three distinct experimental systems (CZ growth, zinc diffusion, oxide precipitation), reducing regression uncertainty relative to single-dataset fits.

37. **Parameterization of Transient Models of Defect Dynamics in Czochralski Silicon Crystal Growth**
    T. Sinno, T. A. Frewen, E. Dornberger, R. Hoelzl, C. Hoess
    *Materials Research Society Symposium Proceedings*, published March 2011 (MRS Copyright 2002).
    Uses Simulated Annealing to robustly parameterize a transient point-defect dynamics model against multiple experimental datasets and multiple objective functions.

38. **Dependence of Ring-Like Distributed Stacking Faults on the Axial Temperature Gradient of Growing Czochralski Silicon Crystals** (ChemInform abstract of item 2)
    E. Dornberger, W. von Ammon
    *ChemInform*, August 2010.
    Abstracting-service reprint/summary of the 1996 original (item 2).

## Notes on scope and completeness

- This list draws on Dornberger's ResearchGate publication record (46 listed works, ~1,435 citations as of retrieval) cross-checked against citing/cited literature in Journal of Crystal Growth, Journal of The Electrochemical Society, Materials Science and Engineering R, and MRS proceedings.
- Excluded as out of scope (not modeling/simulation of crystal growth): later Wacker Polysilicon feedstock/production papers (fluidized-bed reactor pilot production, granular polysilicon patents, seed-material studies for multicrystalline ingots), the "Silicon" encyclopedia chapter (2026, general reference/production overview), and a 1992 laser-diagnostics flame-measurement paper unrelated to crystal growth.
- Several entries (7, 8, 21, 36) are recognized as particularly influential: the 1997 three-code thermal-simulation benchmark, the 1997 PhD thesis, the 2000 *Materials Science and Engineering R* review, and the 2003 multi-dataset point-defect parameterization.
- Author-name conventions in the literature vary between "Erich Dornberger" and "E. Dornberger"; no publications under "Evgenii Dornberger" were located — this appears to be a transliteration/alternate rendering of "Erich," consistent with all located affiliations at Wacker Siltronic/Wacker Chemie AG.
