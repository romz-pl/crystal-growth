# Bibliography: Kaspars Dadzis — Crystal Growth Modeling and Simulation

**Scope.** This bibliography collects the modeling- and simulation-focused publications of Dr. Kaspars Dadzis, currently head of the Junior Research Group "Model Experiments" at the Leibniz-Institut für Kristallzüchtung (IKZ), Berlin. It spans his doctoral and post-doctoral work at Fraunhofer IISB / Freiberg (traveling-magnetic-field directional solidification of multicrystalline silicon), his industrial period at SolarWorld, and his IKZ period (2016–present), including the ERC Starting Grant project **NEMOCRYS** ("Next Generation Multiphysical Models for Crystal Growth Processes," grant agreement No. 851768). Only entries concerned with numerical/mathematical modeling, simulation software, or simulation-validation methodology are included; purely experimental or characterization-only works are excluded unless modeling is a substantive component. Entries are sorted chronologically by year and, within a year, approximately by publication date. DOIs are given where available.
